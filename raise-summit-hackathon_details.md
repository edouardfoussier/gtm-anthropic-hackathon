Sillage
Sillage turns market signals into pipeline and lets you know when your buyers move, before your competitors do.

For this hackathon, you'll need to use Sillage's MCP server:

If you're here with your team, you're all set - no need to confirm anything or go through the app's front end. Head straight to the MCP.

Once your team is set, just one of you creates the workspace at hackathon.getsillage.com and invites the others directly from the app - one person per team is enough, no need for everyone to create one. Rules
1. Tracks & Challenges
You must choose one track:

Track 1: Acquisition Your agent chases new customers: finds the right people, writes outreach they reply to, fills the pipeline.

Here are a few ideas - feel free to explore other things!

Rank target accounts from intent signals
Outreach personal enough to not feel automated (email, LinkedIn, phone)
Multi-channel cadences that A/B test themselves
Surface warm intros in mutual connections and alumni networks
Qualify and route inbound leads on the spot
Track 2: Expansion Your agent grows existing accounts: spots upsell, catches churn early, keeps accounts healthy.

Here are a few ideas - feel free to explore other things!

Track account health via usage, tickets, and sentiment
Catch expansion triggers: new hires, funding, team growth
Spot cross-sell and upsell in usage patterns
See churn coming from early disengagement signals
Track 3: GTM Free-For-All Any idea related to GTM that doesn't fit in the two previous tracks. Go for it ! Hackathons challenge creativity.

Side challenges (you can compete in 0 to all challenges):

Most viral on Linkedin: post about your project, in any format. Most engagement end-of-day wins. You must tag Anthropic, Sillage, FullEnrich, and submit your post in the buzz tab.
Most viral on X: post about your project, in any format. Most engagement 24h later wins. You must tag Anthropic, Sillage, FullEnrich, and include #agenticgtm
Most creative GTM Angle: a unique approach to GTM. Jury vote.
Crowd Favorite: loudest demo wins.
Best use of Gamma.
Best use of Gradium.
2. Judging Criteria
100 points total, four equal criteria of 25 each:

Business impact / problem relevance (25)
Depth of AI & workflow use, Anthropic (25)
Depth of external data use, FullEnrich & Sillage (25)
Presentation quality (25)
3. Presentation format
You'll need to submit:

a 2-minutes demo of your project
the link to your github repo
a short description of your project
The judging will take place in 2 rounds.

1st round:

5 minutes per team:
Pitch (1:30): the problem, your approach plus high-level scheme, expected business impact
Demo (1:30)
Q&A (2:00)
2nd round:

between 8 and 10 finalists will be selected to pitch on stage in front of everyone
finalists will be announced one by one and should be ready to pitch and present their project when called
3 minutes per team:
Pitch (1:30): the problem, your approach plus high-level scheme, expected business impact
Demo (1:30)
no questions
4. Submission rules
You must use Anthropic, FullEnrich AND Sillage for your project.
Build entirely during the event. No prior commits to the repo.
Teams of 3 to 5.
Open-source libs, APIs, and pre-trained models allowed if properly credited.
Submit all code and demos before the deadline to be eligible.
Follow the law, ethical AI practices, and the Code of Conduct. Never use data without consent.
Use partner and sponsor APIs per their terms of service.
Organizers may disqualify any team for rule violations or unfair play.
